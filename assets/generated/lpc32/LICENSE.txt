The art in this directory (style 'lpc32') is Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) work.
Character sheets were composed with the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character
Generator; terrain was cut from the LPC tileset.
  https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator
  https://github.com/OpenGameArt/LiberatedPixelCup
